# Fireworks Storefront Overview

Welcome to the Fireworks storefront. This section focuses on seasonal celebration, high-energy offerings, and streamlined pre-ordering.

## Access & Age Verification

Access to the Fireworks vertical is age-gated:
- **Requirement:** Users must confirm they are **18 years of age or older**.
- **Enforcement:** Similar to the cannabis vertical, an age gate is presented before accessing the storefront, and the event is logged.

## Seasonal Campaigns & Pre-Orders

The Fireworks experience is deeply seasonal:
- **Campaigns:** Real, staff-configured countdowns drive seasonal awareness (e.g., Canada Day, Victoria Day). These are authentic deadlines, not artificial urgency.
- **Pre-Ordering:** Customers can easily reserve holiday bundles.
- **Collection SLAs:** Pre-order confirmation and pick-up updates are sent via SMS, often enforcing a 60-second SLA for real-time responsiveness.
