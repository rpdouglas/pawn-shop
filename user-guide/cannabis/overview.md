# Cannabis Storefront Overview

Welcome to the Cannabis storefront. This section operates under strict compliance rules designed to maintain privacy, discretion, and legal standards.

## Access & Age Verification

Access to the Cannabis vertical is strictly age-gated:
- **Requirement:** Users must confirm they are **19 years of age or older** before accessing any content or inventory.
- **Enforcement:** The age gate is a session-scoped, full-screen interstitial.
- **Audit Logging:** Every age gate pass or fail is recorded securely in the platform's audit logs.

## Discretion & Privacy

The Cannabis experience is built on the **Marie Discretion Test**:
- **No Aggressive Marketing:** The copy is warm, premium, and focused on wellness.
- **Private Browsing:** Filter selections (e.g., mood, category) are not stored in browser history or URLs.
- **Discreet Notifications:** CRM emails and SMS alerts use generic descriptors (e.g., "The Pawn Shop Update" or "Wellness Item") and never expose cannabis-specific terminology.

## Discovery

- **Mood Collections:** Browse by intended effect (Relax, Focus, Social, Ceremony).
- **Product Intelligence:** Detailed wellness profiles for each product (THC/CBD levels, terpene spider charts, genetic lineage).
