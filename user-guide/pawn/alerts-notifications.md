# Alerts & Notifications

Stay updated on new inventory matching your interests and manage your saved items.

## Favourites & Wishlist

You can "heart" any item while browsing to save it to your personal wishlist.

1.  **Add to Favourites:** Tap the heart icon in the item's quick-view or detail page.
2.  **View Favourites:** Access your saved items at any time via the "Favourites" link in the header.
3.  **Real-time Status:** Favourites automatically reflect if an item has been reserved or sold.

## Saved Searches

Get notified immediately when new items matching your specific criteria arrive.

### Creating a Saved Search
1.  Perform a search for an item (e.g., "gold ring").
2.  Tap the **"Save this search"** button in the search results header.
3.  Select your preferred **Alert Method** (SMS or Email).
4.  Confirm to start receiving alerts.

### Managing Searches
Active searches can be toggled on/off or deleted from your user profile settings.

## Notification Centre

The bell icon in the main navigation bar keeps you informed of:
*   **Inventory Matches:** Immediate alerts when a new item matches one of your saved searches.
*   **Item Status Updates:** Changes to items you have favourited or reserved.

Notifications are updated in real-time and can be marked as read once viewed.
