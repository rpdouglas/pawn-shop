# Customer Authentication

Creating an account with The Pawn Shop gives you access to a suite of personalized features, including order tracking, saved searches, and favourites.

## Signing Up & Logging In

- **Email & Password:** You can create an account using a standard email and password.
- **Google SSO:** For a frictionless experience, we support one-click sign-in via Google.

## Account Security (MFA)

We take your security seriously.
- **MFA Enrollment:** While primarily enforced for our administrative staff, we strongly encourage all users to enroll in Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) using an authenticator app.
- **Managing MFA:** You can configure this via your profile settings at `/auth/mfa-enroll`.
