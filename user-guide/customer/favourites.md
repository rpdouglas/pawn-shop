# Saved Searches & Favourites

Never miss a deal on the items you care about most.

## Favourites

See an item you like but aren't ready to pull the trigger?
- **Heart an Item:** Click the heart icon on any item detail page to save it to your Favourites.
- **Quick Access:** Access your favourites list anytime via your user profile dropdown.

## Saved Searches & Alerts

For our power buyers and resellers, the platform acts as your personal scout.
- **Create an Alert:** Perform a search and save it.
- **Real-Time Notifications:** Our system dispatches SMS or email alerts within 60 seconds of a matching item being published.
