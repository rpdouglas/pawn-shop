# Support & Legal

We value your privacy and aim to be transparent in our operations.

## Accessibility

Our storefronts are built with accessibility at the forefront:
- **Mobile First:** Navigation targets are built large (48px+) for easy mobile use.
- **Screen Readers:** Our core flows are spot-checked for VoiceOver and NVDA compatibility.
- View our formal [Accessibility Statement](/accessibility).

## Privacy & Terms

- Your data is managed strictly in accordance with PIPEDA regulations.
- Audit logs securely track sensitive administrative access but explicitly exclude Personal Identifiable Information (PII).
- Please review our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and [Terms of Service](/terms) for comprehensive details.
