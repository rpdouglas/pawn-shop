# Regional Search (Local SEO)

To support **Dale's** search for high-quality cross-border deals, we maintain dynamic regional landing pages optimized for search engine visibility.

## Regional Pages

Our platform automatically generates optimized pages for the following key locations:
- Cornwall, ON
- Massena, NY
- Malone, NY
- Akwesasne
- Rooseveltown, NY
- Hogansburg, NY

These pages are accessible at `/local/:location` (e.g., `/local/massena`).

## Technical Optimization

Each regional page includes:
1.  **JSON-LD LocalBusiness Schema:** Injected into the document `<head>` to provide structured data for Google Search.
2.  **Dynamic Metadata:** Automated updates to the page `<title>` and `<meta name="description">` based on the specific location.
3.  **Cross-Border Trust Content:** Tailored messaging regarding deal verification, serial blacklist checks, and transparent valuation.

## Persona Alignment

- **Dale:** Finds the shop via regional searches and gains confidence from the structured location data and verified deal messaging.
- **Search Engines:** The combination of JSON-LD and semantic content ensures our regional presence is properly indexed.
