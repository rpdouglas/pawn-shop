# Editorial CMS & Brand Narrative

The **Editorial CMS** is the heart of The Pawn Shop's brand identity. It allows staff to manage the Akwesasne identity series, the "Warriors of Akwesasne" features, and the "Finds of the Week" collection.

## Managing Articles

Staff with the `marketing_staff` role can access the CMS at `/admin/articles`.

1.  **Article List:** View all published and draft stories.
2.  **Creation:** Create new stories by assigning a title, slug, and a target view (Pawn, Cannabis, or Fireworks).
3.  **Markdown Support:** The editor supports standard Markdown for rich formatting of the article body.
4.  **SEO Metadata:** Set custom SEO titles and descriptions to ensure our stories are discoverable by customers like **Jordan**.

## The Akwesasne Integrity Gate (Crucial)

To protect the **Makoonsii** persona's trust and ensure cultural authenticity, the CMS implements a mandatory publishing gate for indigenous language content.

- **The Rule:** No article containing Kanien'kéha (Mohawk) language may be published without explicit community review.
- **Enforcement:** If an article contains `[mohawk]` tags, the system will block publication until the **Kanien'kéha Reviewed** checkbox is manually confirmed by a staff member.
- **Audit:** All review and publication events are permanently logged in the [Audit Logs](/admin/audit-logs).

## Finds of the Week

The "Finds of the Week" series showcases high-value items with deep provenance. 

- **Photography Standard:** All featured items must pass the **Marcus Photography Test** (dark luxury standard, well-lit, macro focus) before the article is published.
- **Provenance:** Descriptions should leverage the provenance notes from the inventory record to provide authentic cultural or historical context.
