# Merchandising & Staff Picks

Merchandising at The Pawn Shop is about highlighting the unique, high-quality items in our collection to the right customers.

## Staff Picks

Staff Picks are editorial endorsements that appear on the Pawn homepage and within item details.
- **Curator Voice:** Unlike algorithmic suggestions, Staff Picks should use a first-person curator voice.
- **Adding a Pick:**
  1. Navigate to `/admin/staff-picks`.
  2. Select an item from the list.
  3. Click **Add Staff Pick**.
  4. **Curator Note:** Write a brief note explaining why this item is special (e.g., "The patina on this vintage watch is extraordinary").

## Trending Items

The system automatically tracks engagement and surfaces "Trending" items to customers.
- **Trending Score:** Calculated using a combination of `viewCount` and `enquiryCount`.
- **Automatic Tags:** Items may automatically receive the `just-arrived` tag if listed within the last 48 hours.

## Rare & Limited Items

Managers can manually apply the `rare-find` or `limited-edition` tags to items. These tags should be used sparingly to maintain their integrity and trust with customers like **Marcus**.
