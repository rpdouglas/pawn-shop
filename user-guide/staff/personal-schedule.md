# My Schedule

As a staff member of The Pawn Shop, you can access your personalized work schedule at any time.

## Accessing Your Schedule

Navigate to `/staff/schedule`. This page is protected and requires you to be signed in with your staff account.

## Shift Details

Your schedule includes:
- **Day and Date:** Clearly listed for each shift.
- **Shift Window:** The exact hours you are expected to be on the floor.
- **Assignment:** Your specific location for the shift (e.g., the Pawn desk or the Cannabis lounge).
- **Manager Notes:** Any specific instructions or priorities left for you by the scheduling manager.

## Notifications

While you should check your schedule regularly, any critical changes or reminders will be sent via "The Pawn Shop Update" (following the Marie Discretion Test to ensure your privacy).
