# Cultural & Linguistic Policy — The Pawn Shop

This policy protects the cultural integrity of the Akwesasne community and the Kanien'kéha language.

## 1. The Kanien'kéha Rule
*   **Mandate:** AI (including Antigravity and subagents) is strictly forbidden from generating Kanien'kéha language content.
*   **Enforcement:** Every instance of the language must be flagged for "Manual Community Review."

## 2. Review Sign-off
*   **Mandate:** No Kanien'kéha content can be merged into `main` without an entry in `docs/CULTURAL_LOG.md`.
*   **Enforcement:** The `Linguistic_Auditor` subagent will block PRs missing this sign-off.

## 3. Brand Voice
*   **Mandate:** Use the "Dapper & Debonair" voice. Avoid "Cheap", "Junk", or "Budget".
*   **Enforcement:** Evaluated by the `Brand_Auditor` subagent.
